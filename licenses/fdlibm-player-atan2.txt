The port preserves the original reduction and arithmetic, uses alias-safe
word access and exact hexadecimal constants, and specifies the qualified
JDK17 native NaN payload selection independently of C operand commutation.
It calls the separately attributed private action atan implementation.
OpenJDK17 source is a separate audit input, not the redistributed source of
this permissively licensed port. Source pins and the two normalization rules
used by that audit are in tests/reference/entity/active_tick/math-origin.json.
