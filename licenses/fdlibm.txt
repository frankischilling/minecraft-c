  - Rename the IEEE log entrypoint to mc_math_strict_log and supply a modern
    C17 declaration in mc/core/math/strict.h.
  - Replace fdlibm.h and int-pointer word aliasing with memcpy/uint64_t bit
    extraction and high-word replacement; decode signed words without an
    out-of-range signed cast. Use fixed-width, bounded integer arithmetic.
  - Encode the ten supplied coefficients/scaling constants and the small-path
    one-third coefficient as exact binary64 hexadecimal floating constants.
  - Add explicit braces, formatting, platform/evaluation assertions and the
    admitted positive-finite/strict-FP contract without changing the source
    branch conditions, arithmetic association or return formulas.
  - Preserve the full original algorithm and applicable copyright/permission
    comments. Normalize their indentation/whitespace for this source file.
