The build applies one compiler-compatibility change dated 2026-10-25 in private
copies of alc/effects/chorus.cpp, distortion.cpp, fshifter.cpp and pshifter.cpp.
It passes an explicitly value-initialized
std::array<UpsampleParams, NumLines>{} to mUpsampler.emplace instead of invoking
emplace with no arguments. This preserves the member defaults while avoiding
a Clang 18/libstdc++ 13 constructibility rejection. The archive and supplied
source overrides remain unchanged. cmake/openal_dependencies.cmake records
the exact upstream hashes and replacement used to build the shared library.
